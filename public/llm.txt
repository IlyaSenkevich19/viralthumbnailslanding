## What is ViralThumbnails?

ViralThumbnails is an AI-powered YouTube thumbnail generator that creates high-CTR thumbnails in under 2 minutes. It replaces expensive freelancers ($50–200 per thumbnail) and hours spent in Canva or Photoshop.

## Who is it for?

YouTube creators with 5,000–30,000 subscribers who upload regularly (1–5 videos per week) and want to increase their click-through rate, views, and revenue without spending hours on design.

## How does it work?

1. Paste a YouTube link, upload a video, or describe your idea in a text prompt.
2. Choose from 100+ proven high-CTR templates or upload your own Canva-compatible design.
3. Select or upload your face/avatar. Add hooks, text overlays, and style instructions.
4. AI generates multiple thumbnail variations. Edit, compare, and A/B test them.
5. Export the winning thumbnail or save it to your project library.
