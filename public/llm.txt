## What is ViralThumblify?

ViralThumblify is a web-based YouTube thumbnail studio. Paste a link, upload a video, or describe the idea — then generate multiple thumbnail variants, refine the strongest option, and export.

## Who is it for?

YouTube creators who upload regularly and want faster thumbnail workflows without juggling Canva, freelancers, and multiple tools.

## How does it work?

1. Paste a YouTube URL, upload a video, or enter a text prompt.
2. Pick a preset template and optionally attach a saved face reference.
3. Run the studio pipeline to analyze the source and generate variants.
4. Compare results, refine promising thumbnails, and export the winner.

## Key features (currently in product)

- AI thumbnail generation from YouTube link, video upload, or text prompt
- Preset template library with niche filters
- Saved face references for consistent branding
- Multiple variants per project
- Refine and export finished thumbnails
- Projects saved in your workspace
- Credit ledger with refunds on failed runs
